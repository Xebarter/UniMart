About UniMart
Built for Campus. Powered by Community.

UniMart is a campus marketplace designed to make it easier for students and campus communities to buy, sell, work, offer services, rent things and discover opportunities — all in one place.

We believe universities are more than places to study. They are communities full of skills, businesses, ideas, products and opportunities that often go undiscovered.

UniMart brings these together.

What You Can Do on UniMart
🛍️ Buy & Sell

Find products from people and shops within your campus community, or list things you want to sell.

🛠️ Offer & Find Services

Have a skill? Offer it. Need someone to help? Find them — from tutoring and design to photography, beauty and tech services.

🔑 Rent & List Rentals

Find items you need temporarily or make money by renting out things you own.

💼 Find & Post Gigs

Discover short-term work opportunities or post a gig when you need someone to get a job done.

🏪 Discover Campus Shops

Follow shops you like and keep up with the products and services they offer.

❤️ Save What Interests You

Save listings you're interested in and easily come back to them later.

📰 Campus Magazine

Read stories, insights, events and useful content from the university community — and contribute your own stories for consideration.

Built Around Campus Communities

UniMart is designed with universities in mind.

Instead of searching through disconnected social media groups, chats and pages, UniMart brings commerce, services, work and community content together in one organized campus marketplace.

Whether you're a student selling a laptop, a designer looking for clients, someone searching for a weekend gig, or a student with a story to share — there's a place for you on UniMart.

Our Vision

To become the trusted digital marketplace for university communities, helping students turn what they have, what they know and what they can do into opportunities.
